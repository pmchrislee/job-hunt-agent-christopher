# Job Search Profile

## Target Roles
- Senior Software Engineer

## Work Arrangement
- Flexible — open to onsite, hybrid, or remote

## Target Locations
- San Francisco Bay Area (SF, South Bay, Peninsula, East Bay)
- New York City metro

## Industries & Companies
- High tech (AI, SaaS, fintech, digital health, edtech)

## Compensation
- $200,000+ total comp (base + equity + bonus)

## Hard Constraints
- US work-authorized — no visa sponsorship needed
- No startups below Series B (skip pre-revenue / seed-stage)
- Full-time preferred (W-2)
